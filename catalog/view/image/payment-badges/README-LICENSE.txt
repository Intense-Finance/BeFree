Payment badge marks used in this folder
======================================

1) Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal
   Source: aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-icons (flat-rounded style)
   https://github.com/aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-icons
   License: Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE-aaronfagan-Apache-2.0.txt)

2) Apple Pay, Google Pay, Venmo
   Source: Simple Icons v11.14.0
   https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
   License: CC0 1.0 Universal (see LICENSE-simple-icons-CC0.md)
   Brand names and logos are trademarks of their owners. These marks are
   used only to show accepted payment methods at checkout.

3) Pay Later
   Original typographic badge drawn for this package (not a third-party
   trademark logo). Color #003087 matches the PayPal blue used in (1).

These files are for "accepted methods" display on slopcore.army / BeFree.
Do not change payment settings when uploading this package.

4) Pay Later and the Venmo badge used in the row are original typographic
   marks (word in a rounded rectangle), not third-party logo files.

5) applepay-badge.svg and googlepay-badge.svg are original typographic
   marks (the words “Apple Pay” / “Google Pay” on a rounded rectangle),
   used so the labels stay at or above 14px. The Simple Icons SVG files
   applepay.svg, googlepay.svg, and venmo.svg are kept in this folder for
   license provenance (CC0) but are not the marks rendered in the row.
